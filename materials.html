Finish Comparison

Matte or Gloss?

Two finishes. Two very different visual personalities. Both are professional — but they communicate differently.

Matte Finish

Soft. Refined. Easy to read.

  • Non-reflective — reads clearly under direct lighting
  • Elegant, subdued appearance for premium products
  • Reduces fingerprint visibility on the surface
  • Excellent colour fidelity without shine distortion
  • Writeable with most ballpoint and marker pens

Choose Matte When

Artisan products Premium packaging Professional labels Minimalist brands Photography
Gloss Finish

Vibrant. Reflective. Eye-catching.

  • High-shine surface that pops on shelves
  • Colour saturation amplified for maximum impact
  • Moisture and scratch-resistant protective layer
  • Brighter whites and deeper blacks vs matte
  • Classic product label finish — trusted and familiar

Choose Gloss When

Food & drink labels Promotional stickers Bold branding Retail shelving
Clear & Transparent

Let the Surface Show Through.

Clear labels create a "no-label" look that lets your packaging material speak for itself. Ideal for glass, transparent containers, and premium product presentation where less is more.

Print is applied directly onto transparent film. Where no ink is printed, the underlying surface remains fully visible.

Clear Labels — Best For

Premium glass bottle packaging
Cosmetics and skincare containers
Beverage and food labelling
Minimal brand presentation
Transparent candle and jar labels

Material Is Only Half the Story.

The material sets the foundation. Print quality, cutting precision, and finishing determine whether the final sticker looks as good as the design file intended.

Sharp detail across fine text, gradients, and small logo elements.
