Choose the Sticker That Fits the Job.

Every application asks for something different. A bottle needs a label that survives condensation. A carton needs a barcode that scans first try. A storefront window needs vinyl that peels away clean. Below, we've broken down our core product lines by shape, material, finish and use — so you can match the right sticker to the right job before you upload a single file.

Product Labels

Your label is the first thing a customer reads before they've read anything else. We print labels that hold their color, resist moisture and stay put through shipping, shelf time and daily handling — for jars, bottles, boxes and pouches alike.

Best for
Bottles, jars, boxes & retail packaging
Available shapes
Rectangle, square, round, custom die-cut
Material options
Paper, vinyl, kraft, clear film
Finish options
Matte, gloss, satin

Cosmetics, food & beverage, candles, skincare

02 — Inventory & Logistics

Barcode Stickers

Built for scanners, not just eyes. Our barcode stickers print crisp, high-contrast codes that read cleanly the first time, every time — on cartons, shelf edges, storage bins and shipped products.

Best for
Inventory tracking & retail logistics
Available shapes
Rectangle, small square
Material options
Thermal paper, matte vinyl, polypropylene
Finish options
Matte, scanner-optimized
Typical applications
Warehousing, retail POS, shipping, asset tags

03 — Interior & Signage

Wall Decals

Turn a blank wall, window or storefront into a statement. Our decals are cut for scale — from a single logo to a full-wall installation — using materials designed to apply cleanly and remove without residue when it's time for something new.

Best for
Walls, windows, storefronts & interiors
Available shapes
Custom-cut logos, patterns, full-wall murals
Material options
Removable vinyl, matte vinyl, static cling
Finish options
Matte, low-glare satin, textured
Typical applications
Offices, retail storefronts, studios, event spaces
