Quick Answers

Common Questions.

How quickly do you turn around orders?
Standard production is 2–4 business days from artwork approval. Rush options are available — mention your deadline when you submit your inquiry and we'll confirm availability.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
We work with orders as small as a single sheet. For best unit pricing, orders of 250+ tend to offer the most value. We'll include a pricing breakdown with your quote.
What file format do you need for artwork?
We accept PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, and JPG. Vector formats (PDF, AI, EPS, SVG) give the sharpest results. All artwork should be set up at print size, 300 DPI minimum.
Can I get a physical material sample before ordering?
Yes. Request a free swatch envelope through the inquiry form above. We'll post a selection of materials and finishes to you so you can assess them in person before committing to a run.
